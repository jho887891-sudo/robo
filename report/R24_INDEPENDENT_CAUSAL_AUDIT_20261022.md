## Concrete issues

1. **CRITICAL: TRAIN split is not TRAIN seen**. `tools/r24_frame_manifest.py:119-145` selects 64 Push-T and 72 Stack Bowls frames from TRAIN *episodes*, not from the 2000 samples actually consumed by the optimizer. `tools/r24_fit_test.py:105-135` then calls them TRAIN and compares CE means to DEV. The checkpoint's `sampler.json` stores the permutation and position; `training/r15_resume.py:91-156` is authoritative for the actual consumption order. From 2000/61,171 (3.27% of an epoch), uniformly selected 136 TRAIN frames would *on average* contain just 4.45 gradient-exposed samples, not 136. Exact overlap must be reconstructed, not assumed. A TRAIN/DEV gap (or lack thereof) in this manifest is therefore **not** an overfit or training memorisation test. Added `tools/r24_seen_exposure_audit.py` and five CPU unit tests. Only a full-state validated checkpoint and its exact task schedule can establish membership; tool fails closed on missing/mismatched hashes. A follow-up must compare **actual seen**, **TRAIN-unseen** and **DEV** at the same checkpoint, paired by episode/phase, preserving the manifest.

2. **CRITICAL: Bayes optimality was not established.** `mission/r24/residual_learnability.json` records unconditional `abs(mean(R)) / mean(abs(R)) ≈ 0.20`. A small unconditional mean cannot establish that `E[R | 3RGB, 42D, task]` is zero. For squared error the Bayes estimator is the *conditional mean*; for absolute error it is a *conditional median*. A bimodal left/right pushing policy can have mean zero but remain visually predictable. `tools/r24_learnability.py` tests a ridge linear regressor on **28D [S,V]**, not the model's 42D [S,V,Acc] nor any RGB or language features (the locally created `feat` variable is unused). Negative DEV R² on 64/72 TRAIN frames proves at most poor generalisation of this one weak probe; it cannot upper-bound conditional learnability from VLA multimodal input. Do not relabel as ground-truth H4.

3. **CRITICAL: contrary 7B fit experiment exists.** `evidence/r14_overfit_20261020T200030Z/raw.log` reports a fixed-sample teacher-forced CE from 14.8141 at step0 to **0.1250 at step100**, `OVERFIT_SIGNAL_PASS`. This shows at least the R14 recipe can fit repeated supervised targets on fixed frames; it neither proves generalisation nor validates M2. It directly prevents an "in-principle cannot fit action labels" conclusion.

4. **HIGH: R15 counts, learning-rate attribution, and missing-source audit are inaccurate.** `evidence/r15_stage300_20261021T034102Z/stage_evidence.json` records **19,127** TRAIN frames from **64** episodes for the R15 sealed run, not the 25,313 frames/80 episodes that R24 borrows from M2 split; R15 epoch fraction at 1000 should be **0.0523**, not 0.0395, assuming that checkpoint's train list is authoritative. The raw R15 step0→300 logfile is committed at `evidence/r15_stage300_20261021T034102Z/raw_train.log`, contrary to "missing/no raw logs" in R24. The `run_r15` path at `training/r11_train_pushT_r42.py:652` uses hard-coded `MultiStepLR(milestones=[1000,2500],gamma=0.1)`, not the `--lr_milestone` default at CLI line1044 (used in another path). 5. **HIGH: fair SAFE evaluation is valuable but not deployment-equivalent.** `mission/r24/fair_audit_fit.json` uses `per-task normalizer payload pmin/pmax`; R24 explicitly records **no M2 checkpoint deployment metadata**, and `tools/r24_fair_audit.py` does not evaluate the serving rate limiter. Clipping/bounds dominate the score for some strata. Calculate fraction and magnitude of **ground-truth joint targets and current states outside limits** per task/split; explicitly measure irreducible clamping error `mean|clip(A,amin,amax)-A|`, compare true deployment limits and gripper/rate policy. Until metadata bridge is fixed, call metrics "matched offline approximate SAFE", never "actual M2 deployment physical error."

6. **HIGH: R24 headline CI is from the 8-frame archive, not the new 284-frame fit audit.** `mission/r24/fair_audit_devstep2000.json` uses `evidence/dev_eval_v2_aligned.npz`, **8 DEV frames/task**, despite the report also describing 284 selected TRAIN+DEV frames. Its model-CV CIs cross zero, which is *failure to reject a difference*, not formal **statistical equivalence**; equivalence needs prespecified practical margin and appropriate CI. The 284-frame archive `mission/r24/fair_audit_fit.json` uses different motion strata and yields different absolute L1. It includes only 6 DEV episodes for Push-T and 4 DEV episodes for Stack Bowls.
